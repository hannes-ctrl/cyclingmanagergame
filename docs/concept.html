Cycling Manager

Concept for a pure cycling management game · working title Radmanager · as of October 2026, version 0.6.1 alpha, 0.7.0 in progress

A clickable rough sketch from the start of the project; the real app has grown past it, but the core loop is the same. The sidebar switches the view, a rider's name opens the rider profile, and "Continue" plays through the core loop (inbox → race preparation → radio call → race report). All values, wages, programmes and results are made-up sample data; in the game they come from the WorldDB.
